独学 · dokugaku
An agent-powered hub for self-learners
01 / name
独doku · alone, by oneself
学gaku · learning, study

独学 (dokugaku) is the Japanese word for self-study.

It names exactly what the platform is for: one person, one topic, and the discipline to keep going. The agent teaches, quizzes and grades, but the learning stays yours.

A book, a certification, a tool's documentation, a course: everything you study is a topic, and SLP builds the full loop around it.

02 / why

The method comes built in.

The knowledge is nothing if you cannot externalize in your own way. You need a method where you have an input and an output channel with the appropiate feedback.

Everything stays on your disk.

No need for internet connection, no need to share your private learning session. You are the owner, as it should be.

03 / the learning loop

The study loop consists of acquiring knowledge from any source, internalizing it with logic, being able to express it, and obtaining feedback.

01 / principles

Four Japanese design ideas describe what SLP already does. They are a vocabulary for decisions, not a visual theme.

簡素
kansosimplicity

Remove until only what is needed is left.

// one font, no shadows, no gradients
間
manegative space

Empty space is part of the design, not a gap to fill.

// the ~ buffer, the clear space around the mark
渋い
shibuiquiet beauty

Understated. Nothing is decorated for its own sake.

// hierarchy by lightness, not size
静寂
seijakucalm

The interface stays silent while you study.

// chrome lives in two bars; content stays quiet
02 / logo
Clear space

Keep empty space of at least ¼ of the mark's height on every side. This is ma: the space belongs to the logo.

Minimum size
mark 16px
stacked 96px
horizontal 180px

Below these widths the Latin line stops reading. Use the 独 mark alone.

Don't
  • ✕ add color
  • ✕ stretch or rotate
  • ✕ add shadow or glow
  • ✕ crop or crowd
  • ✕ retype the lettering
