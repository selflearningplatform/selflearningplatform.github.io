# learning.md

The topic's memory: what you've shown you know, and what you got wrong and corrected. Every skill reads it before writing.

```shape
# Learning — <topic>
## Mission
## Glossary
## Record
### 0002 — Believed "scalable" implied "distributed" — corrected
### 0001 — Source choice: ...
```

- **mission**: why you study this, from `slp-init`
- **glossary**: terms you can already use
- **record**: numbered entries: confirmed floor, corrected misconceptions, source choices
